# WBCD asset notes

This folder stores photos and videos from the WBCD event. These notes preserve
the real context for later blog writing.

## Assets

- `wbcd-visit-agilex-dual-arm-platform.jpeg`
  - A photo taken while visiting the exhibition area.
  - Shows an AGILE-X dual-arm tabletop platform.

- `wbcd-3am-pre-competition-debugging-walkthrough.mov`
  - Shot around 3 AM before the competition.
  - Many teams were still debugging equipment overnight.
  - Useful for describing the pre-competition atmosphere and the intensity of
    onsite preparation.

- `wbcd-award-ceremony-zhuo-xu-deepmind-talk.jpeg`
  - Shot during the award ceremony.
  - Shows a short report by Zhuo Xu from Google DeepMind.

- `wbcd-training-data-collection-workstation.jpeg`
  - The workstation used by our team to collect training data.
  - Useful when explaining the data collection and preparation workflow.

- `wbcd-other-team-clothes-hanging-demo.mov`
  - A short clip of another team's robot handling clothes.
  - This was not our team's work and not our competition track.
  - Use only as an observation of other teams' embodied manipulation setups.

- `wbcd-visit-humanoid-lift-frame-setup.jpeg`
  - A photo taken while visiting the exhibition area.
  - Shows a humanoid robot mounted on a lift/support frame.

- `wbcd-other-team-logistics-packing-match.mov`
  - Another team's match scene.
  - This was not our team's work and not our competition track.
  - Use only as a reference to the competition environment, not as our own run.

- `wbcd-competition-badge-and-markers.jpeg`
  - A quick photo of my competition badge.
  - Also shows the logistics packing task marker sheets.

- `wbcd-global-first-place-certificate.jpeg`
  - Our certificate for global first place in the ICRA WBCD Logistics Packing
    track.
  - WBCD has track-level global first-place awards, not one overall global
    first-place award for the whole event.
  - Prize note: the team won multiple robot arms with total value above USD
    10,000.

- `wbcd-main-stage-casual-snapshot.jpeg`
  - A casual photo of the main stage and robot display area.

- `wbcd-first-teleoperation-success.mov`
  - Our team's first successful teleoperation run.
  - Useful for explaining the starting point of our system debugging process.

- `wbcd-backdrop-casual-snapshot.jpeg`
  - A casual photo of the WBCD event backdrop.
